# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully receiving constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Doxing (publishing private information without permission).
- Any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting Procedures
If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it via email to: [INSERT_REPORTING_EMAIL_ADDRESS].

All reports will be reviewed by the project maintainers. We are committed to maintaining confidentiality for the reporter and will conduct a thorough, objective investigation into all reported incidents.

## Enforcement and Governance
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

### Enforcement Guidelines
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior will be investigated. The team is obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter.

Possible responses include:
1. A formal warning.
2. Temporary suspension of participation rights.
3. Permanent ban from community spaces.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub repositories, issue trackers, chat channels) and when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
